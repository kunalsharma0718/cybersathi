what is front end developer?

A Frontend Developer is a professional who specializes in creating the user-facing part of a website-everything a person sees and interacts with in their browser.They are the architects of the digital experience,responsiblefpr turning a static design into a living , breathing,nd interactive webpage

Their primaryjob is to use HTML,CSS and JavaScript to build the visual elements , layouts , buttons and animations that make a website functional and enjoyable to use.
